# Support Skill
# Loaded on-demand when the SupportAgent handles a customer query.

## Knowledge Base

### How Gemini Links Work
- Gemini links are invite URLs that grant access to Google's Gemini AI.
- Each link is single-use — once assigned to a user, it cannot be reused.
- Links are valid as long as the inviter's subscription is active.

### Credit System
- Resellers purchase credits from the admin.
- Each link dispensed deducts 1 credit from the reseller's balance.
- Credits do not expire and are non-refundable.

### Troubleshooting
- "Link not working" → Check if the link was already used or if the inviter's subscription expired.
- "Need more links" → Reseller must contact admin to purchase more credits.
- "Wrong link delivered" → Contact admin with your phone number and the link you received.
